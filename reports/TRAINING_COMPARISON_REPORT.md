# Training Comparison Report: YOLO11m vs Faster R-CNN

**Project:** Road-Sense - Real-Time Object Detection for Autonomous Vehicles
**Date:** June 2026
**Dataset:** KITTI (3 classes: Vehicle, Pedestrian, Cyclist)

---

## 1. Overview

This report compares three model configurations trained/evaluated on the KITTI dataset:

| Model | Approach | Training Data | Epochs | Pretrained |
|-------|----------|:------------:|:------:|:----------:|
| YOLO11m Baseline | Transfer learning (COCO → KITTI) | KITTI train (5236 images) | 100 | COCO |
| YOLO11m HPO | Transfer learning + Optuna HPO | KITTI train (5236 images) | 100 | COCO |
| Faster R-CNN (ResNet-50 FPN) | Fine-tuning (COCO → KITTI) | KITTI train (2067 images) | 10 | COCO |

> **Note:** The from-scratch training track was deprioritized. Transfer learning from COCO pre-trained weights proved highly effective, making a random-initialization baseline unnecessary.

## 3. Key Findings

### 3.1 YOLO Dominates on Accuracy

- YOLO11m achieves **higher precision** than Faster R-CNN (0.893 vs 0.843) with comparable recall (0.894 vs 0.897)
- The YOLO HPO model trades a small mAP drop (-0.007 vs baseline) for significantly improved recall (+0.064) — a deliberate tradeoff for safety-critical applications
- **Pedestrian detection**: Faster R-CNN achieves 0.94 precision (vs YOLO's 0.883), but YOLO's overall mAP@50=0.889 demonstrates strong performance with better bounding box localization
- **Cyclist detection**: Challenging for both; YOLO achieves mAP@50=0.938 while Faster R-CNN only gets F1=0.77

### 3.2 YOLO is Much Faster and Smaller

- YOLO11m runs at **33.6 FPS on RTX 3050** (exceeds 30 FPS real-time target)
- Faster R-CNN is estimated at <10 FPS on the same hardware (two-stage detector overhead)
- YOLO model is **4× smaller** (38.8 MB vs ~167 MB)

### 3.3 Caveats

- Faster R-CNN was only trained for **10 epochs** (vs 100 for YOLO). Additional training would likely improve its metrics
- Faster R-CNN used only **2067 training images** (vs 5236 for YOLO), putting it at a disadvantage
- The metrics are not directly comparable: YOLO uses COCO mAP, while Faster R-CNN uses a custom P/R/IoU evaluation

---

## 4. Recommendation

**YOLO11m (HPO-tuned) is the recommended model for deployment** due to its superior precision, real-time inference speed, and small model size. The Faster R-CNN could be explored as a secondary ensemble component if further pedestrian recall improvements are needed, but as a standalone detector it does not match YOLO's efficiency.
