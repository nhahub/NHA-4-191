📋 Common Misclassifications

  • Vehicle → Pedestrian: Rare — typically tall vehicles (trucks) with pedestrian-like proportions
  • Pedestrian → Vehicle: Occurs when pedestrians are very close to vehicles; occlusion causes confusion
  • Cyclist → Pedestrian: Most common error — cyclists viewed from the side lose the bicycle profile
  • Cyclist → Vehicle: Motorcycles/mopeds at distance resemble small vehicles
